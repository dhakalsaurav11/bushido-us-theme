Built in Kathmandu. Worn your way.

Streetwear shaped by Japanese folklore and the energy of the city we call home.

A Kathmandu point of view.

Our point of view is local and outward-looking. The streets around us, the people who wear Bushido and the visual worlds that keep pulling us back all shape the work.

We make clothing with a clear identity: expressive enough to be noticed, considered enough to become part of everyday life.

Folklore, translated.

Dragons, sakura, Ryujin and the idea of mushin run through our collections. We translate those references through silhouette, layering, colour and graphic placement rather than treating them as costume.

The result is clothing that carries a story without telling you how to wear it.

Made for repeat wear.

A garment still has a job to do. We focus on silhouettes that layer easily, graphics that reward a closer look and pieces that feel personal long after the first wear.

Each drop adds another chapter, while the people wearing it decide what that chapter becomes.
